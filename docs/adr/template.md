# ADR-NNN — Title

- **Status**: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNN
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders**: —

## Context

What is the issue we're seeing that motivates this decision? What forces are
at play (technical, product, team, cost)? Two or three paragraphs at most —
link out to design docs for depth.

## Decision

The decision, stated in the active voice ("We will …"). Include the
alternatives that were seriously considered and one line each on why they
were rejected.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what new obligations exist
(runbooks, revisit triggers, follow-up ADRs). Honest costs included —
a consequences section with only upsides is marketing, not engineering.
